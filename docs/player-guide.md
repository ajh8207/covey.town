# Graveyard Shift Player Guide

Graveyard Shift is a multiplayer game where Crewmates complete tasks and identify Impostors, while Impostors eliminate Crewmates and avoid being discovered.

## Roles

- **Crewmates:** Complete assigned tasks, report bodies, and identify Impostors.
- **Impostors:** Eliminate Crewmates, sabotage ship systems, and use fake tasks to avoid suspicion.
- **Ghosts:** Continue supporting their team's objectives after being eliminated, according to their role.
- **Voters:** Participate in meetings, discuss suspicious behavior, and vote for a suspected Impostor or skip.

## Tasks

Crewmates complete tasks throughout the map, including puzzles, wiring, and data transfers. Some tasks require visiting multiple rooms. Impostors can perform fake tasks, but these do not increase the Crewmates' shared task progress.

## Meetings and Voting

Players can report dead bodies or call emergency meetings. During meetings, players share information, discuss suspicious behavior, and vote to eliminate a suspected Impostor or skip voting.

## Sabotage

Impostors can sabotage ship systems, such as lights, communications, or critical systems. Crewmates must work together to resolve emergencies before time runs out.

## Winning Conditions

- **Crewmates win** by completing all required tasks or eliminating all Impostors.
- **Impostors win** by eliminating enough Crewmates or causing a critical sabotage to fail, if implemented.

## Winning with Information

Pay attention to player movements, task activity, and suspicious behavior. Share observations during meetings and use evidence to make informed decisions. Impostors should use deception and sabotage strategically to avoid detection.

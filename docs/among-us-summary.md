## 1. Rooms

The Skeld map includes nine main rooms: Cafeteria, MedBay, Admin, Navigation, Security, O2, Reactor, Electrical, and Communications.

**Communications:** The Communications room contains communication equipment and is associated with tasks such as Download Data and Upload Data. If the impostor sabotages Communications, the crew's task list and task progress indicators may become unavailable, making it harder to track task completion until the sabotage is fixed.

## 2. Tasks

The Skeld includes a variety of tasks that crewmates must complete around the map. Examples include:

- Fixing wiring
- Swiping a card
- Calibrating the distributor
- Charting a course
- Cleaning the O2 filter
- Cleaning a vent
- Clearing asteroids
- Diverting power
- Priming shields
- Stabilizing steering
- Unlocking manifolds
- Uploading data
- Aligning engine output
- Emptying garbage
- Inspecting a sample
- Starting the reactor
- Submitting a scan

These tasks involve different types of interactions, including solving puzzles, entering inputs, following sequences, and completing actions at specific locations.

## 3. Sabotage

Impostors can trigger sabotage events, such as turning off the lights, causing Reactor or O2 emergencies, or disabling Communications. Some sabotages require crewmates to cooperate or respond quickly to resolve the emergency.

## 4. Meetings and Voting

Players can report dead bodies or call emergency meetings, subject to the game's rules and settings. During meetings, players discuss suspicious behavior and decide whom to vote for. Players can vote for a specific person or skip voting. Discussion and voting may have configurable time limits.

## 5. Game Settings

The host can configure several game settings, including:

- Player movement speed
- Impostor vision
- Kill cooldown
- Task counts
- Meeting discussion and voting times
- Number of impostors

These settings affect the game's difficulty and pace.
